## What We're Building

A **scalable, privacy-first social media platform** that competes with Meta/Instagram by offering:

1. **Superior Safety** - AI-powered content moderation (95%+ accuracy)
2. **Better Discovery** - Intelligent recommendations instead of engagement-maximizing algorithms
3. **Creator-Friendly** - Generous revenue sharing (70/30 split)
4. **User-Centric** - Privacy-first design, data transparency
5. **Real-Time** - Sub-second WebSocket updates for instant engagement

**Target Market:** 
- Tech-savvy users disillusioned with Meta/Twitter
- Content creators seeking better platforms
- Enterprise users wanting workplace social networking
- Privacy-conscious users globally

## AI Integration

### Core AI Capabilities

1. **Content Moderation (Real-Time)**
   - 95%+ accuracy in violation detection
   - <500ms processing per post
   - Cost: $0.001 per post
   - Detects: Hate speech, harassment, NSFW, spam, misinformation

2. **Recommendations (Daily Batch + Real-Time)**
   - 20%+ click-through rate
   - 70%+ topic diversity
   - Cold start handling for new users
   - Cost: $0.0002 per user per day

3. **Smart Replies**
   - 30%+ acceptance rate
   - Context-aware suggestions
   - Multi-language support
   - Cost: $0.005 per suggestion

4. **User Safety Monitoring**
   - Harassment detection
   - Suicide/self-harm prevention
   - Toxicity scoring
   - Real-time escalation

5. **Trend Analysis**
   - Real-time trend detection (5-min updates)
   - Prediction accuracy: 75%+
   - Emerging topic discovery
   - Risk detection (misinformation, hatred)

### AI Cost Model

**At 10M Users:**
- Moderation: $5K/day
- Recommendations: $2K/day
- Smart replies: $500/day
- Safety: $1K/day
- Trends: $300/day
- **Total: ~$90K/month** (~$1.08M/year)

**Cost per User: $0.10/month** (vs. revenue of $1.00/month at $10M MAU)

## Risks & Mitigation

### Top 3 Risks

**Risk #1: Market Saturation / Competition**
- *Impact:* High user acquisition cost, low growth
- *Likelihood:* High (Meta will copy features)
- *Mitigation:* 
  - First-mover advantage in privacy+safety combo
  - Strong community lock-in early
  - Clear positioning vs. competitors
  - 18-month lead time before competition responds

**Risk #2: Creator Exodus / Adoption**
- *Impact:* Can't build critical mass
- *Likelihood:* Medium
- *Mitigation:*
  - Generous revenue sharing (70/30)
  - Dedicate creator relations team
  - Creator partnerships from day 1
  - Better analytics/tools than competitors

**Risk #3: Scaling Technical Challenges**
- *Impact:* Service outages, poor experience at scale
- *Likelihood:* Medium (proven architecture helps)
- *Mitigation:*
  - Hire experienced scaling engineers
  - Load testing at 10x expected scale
  - Multi-region architecture
  - Observability/monitoring from day 1
